3. 3D Printing Safety & Material Guidelines

  • Food Contact & Cookie Cutters: Standard FDM 3D prints contain microscopic layer lines that can trap bacteria. When printing our cookie cutter STL files, use food-safe filament, hand-wash only in cool or lukewarm soapy water (never place PLA in a dishwasher, as PLA deforms above 55°C / 130°F), or use a thin layer of plastic wrap between dough and cutter.
  • Thermal Resistance (Vehicle & Outdoor Use): For vehicle cup expanders, can holders, and outdoor planters, we recommend printing in PETG, ABS, or ASA rather than standard PLA to prevent warping in warm interiors or direct sunlight.
